# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

OpenSpace contributors and maintainers commit to a harassment-free, welcoming community regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability. Be respectful, assume good intent while addressing concrete impact, accept constructive feedback, and keep technical disagreement focused on the work.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, deliberate disclosure of private information, sustained disruption, and retaliation are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through a repository maintainer. If no private contact is available, open an issue containing only “Private conduct contact required.” Do not include personal or sensitive details publicly. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participation according to severity and context.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project. Enforcement decisions should protect reporters' privacy, avoid conflicts of interest, and document the minimum necessary facts.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
